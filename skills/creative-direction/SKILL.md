# Creative Direction

Create:
- Brand Idea
- One-line explanation
- Why it is strategically relevant
- Creative North Star
- Visual Tension
- 5–8 Creative / Visual Keywords
- 2–3 Creative Territories when exploration is useful

A Brand Idea must inspire communication, design, experience and activation. It must not simply repeat the purpose or positioning.

Useful tension examples:
Traditional × Contemporary
Technical × Human
Premium × Accessible
Cultural × Global
Playful × Precise
Editorial × Bold
Minimal × Expressive

Choose only tensions justified by the strategy.
