# SEAT19E Brand OS Builder

**Version:** 1.7.0  
**Creator:** Eugene Park / SEAT19E  
**Status:** Private repository

SEAT19E Brand OS Builder is a Korean-first brand strategy, visual identity, and execution-ready toolkit playbook system designed for workshops, founders, executives, marketers, and creative teams.

## What it does

The Builder guides a participant through a one-question-at-a-time strategic interview and turns the answers into a connected Brand OS:

1. Brand strategy discovery
2. Brand identity synthesis
3. Moodboard and visual identity direction
4. Brand Identity Board
5. AI-ready brand instructions
6. Category-adaptive Toolkit Playbook
7. Asset Pairing Guide
8. Rollout / activation structure
9. Visual PDF or presentation-ready output when supported by the host environment

## Core flow

`DISCOVER → DEFINE → CODIFY → ACTIVATE → VISUALIZE → TOOLKIT → DELIVER`

The interview uses 12 strategic stages:

1. Brand Overview
2. Purpose
3. Vision
4. Target Audience
5. Customer Insight
6. Positioning
7. Brand Promise
8. Personality
9. Verbal & Visual Identity
10. Non-Negotiable Principles
11. Reason to Believe
12. Strategic Priorities

## Toolkit Playbook architecture

The execution-ready playbook may include:

- Brand Foundation
- Brand / Product / IP System
- Creative Concept
- Strategic Objective
- Strategic Direction
- Identity & Visual System
- Toolkit Assets Overview
- Category-specific Asset Modules
- Asset Pairing Guide
- Channel / Format Guide
- Rollout Timeline / Activation Calendar
- Output Inventory / Delivery Summary
- Governance & Usage
- Pending / Unverified Items

Asset categories adapt to the business type. F&B, physical product, consulting, agency, software, retail, hospitality, education, entertainment and other categories should not be forced into the same asset taxonomy.

## Strategic and design references

The Builder includes original applied syntheses informed by established branding and design literature, including work associated with Seth Godin, Kevin Roberts, David Aaker, Kevin Lane Keller, Jean-Noël Kapferer, Marty Neumeier, Byron Sharp, Alina Wheeler, Rob Meyerson, Catharine Slade-Brooking, Holly Becker and Simon Seivewright, together with general design principles.

These files do **not** reproduce book chapters, proprietary diagrams, protected page layouts or long copyrighted passages. They translate high-level ideas into original operational guidance for the Builder.

## Evidence policy

The Builder separates:

- **CONFIRMED** — decisions explicitly confirmed by the participant
- **FOUNDER-PROVIDED** — factual claims supplied by the participant
- **PROPOSED** — newly generated strategy, naming, slogan or design recommendations
- **NEEDS VALIDATION** — audience, customer or market hypotheses requiring research
- **UNVERIFIED** — claims without adequate supporting evidence

The system must not invent awards, clients, performance results, partnerships, research findings, certifications or completed deliverables.

## Visual production

A moodboard is exploratory. An identity board is prescriptive.

The Brand Identity Board should connect strategy to:

- wordmark / mark direction
- color system
- typography
- imagery
- layout and spacing
- graphic devices
- key visual
- practical touchpoint mockups

Approved assets always take precedence over generated proposals.

## Workshop privacy

Participants should not enter confidential company information, unreleased financial information, private customer data, passwords, trade secrets or other sensitive information in a public workshop.

## Installation / use

This repository contains the source structure of the ChatGPT plugin/skill. Availability and installation behavior depend on the host environment and its current plugin support.

The source can also be adapted into a standalone web application. Host-specific image generation, PDF creation, authentication and storage require separate implementation.
