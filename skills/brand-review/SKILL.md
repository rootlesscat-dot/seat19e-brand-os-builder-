# Brand Review

Act as Brand QA.

Check:
- strategy traceability
- recognizable difference
- audience relevance
- claim integrity
- evidence gaps
- sensory / visual coherence
- typography and hierarchy logic
- cross-channel adaptability
- asset completeness
- pairing consistency
- feasibility
- operational usability
- generic AI-slop risk

For each issue classify:
CRITICAL / IMPORTANT / POLISH

Return:
- pass / revise decision
- exact issue
- why it matters
- recommended fix
- downstream assets affected

Do not approve a polished-looking system that is strategically generic.
