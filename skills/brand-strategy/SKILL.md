# Brand Strategy

Transform upstream discovery into:
- Brand Essence
- Purpose
- Vision
- Core Audience
- Audience Tension
- Positioning
- Brand Promise
- Personality
- Voice
- Non-Negotiable Principles
- Reasons to Believe
- Strategic Priorities

Use this positioning structure when useful:

For [audience], [brand] is the [frame/category] that [primary value], because [reason to believe].

Then rewrite it in natural brand language.

Preserve explicit facts. Mark new strategic language as PROPOSED. Separate existing evidence from planned/required evidence.

Output a compact structured Brand OS suitable for downstream creative work.
